# Summary — Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct (main), Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct (replication). Numbers: results/RESULTS.md, results/verdicts.json

1. **Best layer:** steering layer j* = 18 of 32 (pre-registered rule: best D1→D2 transfer on D2 *train*, mid-depth window); Qwen j* = 24 of 36.
2. **H1 supported, but uninformative:** D1 held-out accuracy 1.00 [1.00, 1.00] at j*, 22/22 mid-late layers pass. A TF-IDF model on the raw prompt text also gets 1.00, and a probe names which of 10 instruction paraphrases was used (0.99).
3. **H2 (headline) supported:** the D1 (instruction) direction ranks emotional over neutral *content* (D2, no instructions) with AUROC 0.96 [0.93, 0.99]. It beats 1,000 random directions (p = 0.008) but not label-shuffled D1 directions (p = 0.16); 4% of 24 irrelevant-contrast directions do as well (instruction-present-vs-absent: 0.98).
4. **Confounds/controls:** negation test ("Do not be warm…") 1.00 for the direction vs 0.00 for the text model, so it follows instruction *meaning*, not tone words. H&L control task 0.78 [0.69, 0.86] → selectivity 0.22. Length-only AUROC on D2 0.52; transfer after removing length 0.96.
5. **Formats (D3):** content transfer holds for JSON (0.96) and plain completion (0.87) but fails for multiple choice (0.43 [0.33, 0.52]).
6. **D1 vs D2 directions are nearly orthogonal:** cosine 0.21 (shuffled-label 95% band ±0.27). **Deconfounding** (projecting out a rank-12 format/paraphrase/instruction subspace, 23% of the D1 direction) barely moves transfer: 0.96 → 0.92.
7. **H3 supported:** at c = +4, D1 steering minus random = +0.90 [0.65, 1.15] judge points (1–10), task completion 1.00; at c = +8, +4.12 [3.82, 4.40]. Negative c barely lowers emotion (−0.28 at c = −8): the baseline is already near the floor.
8. **H4 not supported:** the D2 (content) direction steers much less: D2 − D1 = −0.88 [−1.13, −0.63] at +4; D2 − random = +0.02 [−0.12, 0.17] at +4, +0.50 [0.22, 0.82] at +8. D1 minus format subspace steers like D1 (+0.33 [0.05, 0.65] vs D1 at +4).
9. **Costs:** perplexity (unsteered model, geo. mean) 1.19 at c = 0 → 1.47 at D1 +4 → 3.70 at D1 +8 (random +8: 1.39). Task completion stays 1.00 for D1; D2 at +8 drops to 0.90.
10. **Side effects (exploratory):** D1 +8 removes bullet lists (0.85 → 0.20) as text turns into gushing prose; D2 +8 brings refusals (judge 0.10) and apologies (0.08); D1 and random: none.
11. **Small model agrees on steering, not on transfer specificity:** Qwen H3 +1.37 [1.10, 1.65], H4 not supported, D1→D2 AUROC 0.84 [0.79, 0.90] but not above random directions (p = 0.11); deconfounding cuts Qwen transfer to 0.68. Qwen D2 +8 is a "comfort/support" mode: task completion 0.55, refusals 0.23.
12. **Judge check:** no human labels yet. Versus a second LLM rater (Claude Opus 5.5, blind, n = 40): Spearman 0.80, within ±1 point on 85%, judge scores 1.1 points higher on average.
13. **Pre-registered verdict:** Llama "emotion direction" (H2 strong + specific, H3 supported); Qwen "mixed" (H2-specific fails).
14. **Honest one-line verdict: mixed** (my exploratory reading; it is stricter than the pre-registered rule, which ignored H4 and the shuffled-label null). The instruction-derived direction does read emotional content and does push outputs toward emotional tone, but the direction learned from emotional content itself barely steers and points elsewhere, so what we found looks more like an "act warm" (style/persona) direction than a general emotion direction.
